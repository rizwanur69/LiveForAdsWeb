Why not one currency?

Offerwalls know the price

A partner pays an exact dollar amount per completed task, so UP can convert at a fixed rate with no guesswork.

Ad revenue moves

Sponsor-ad income changes from season to season. Sharing the pool by each person's AP keeps the payout fair at any size.

Earn both, keep them separate

Your Profile shows each balance live, with a 7-day chart of AP and UP activity.
