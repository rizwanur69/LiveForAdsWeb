LFA token

Why, how and when we may launch an LFA token

A plain-language plan. No hype, no date we cannot keep, and no promise of value. Just the reasons, the stages, and the conditions that must be met first.

Not a current commitment

No LFA token exists today. This page describes an intention to explore one. Plans, names, supply, timing and even the decision itself may change. Nothing here is an offer to sell anything or investment advice. Earning AP and UP today does not guarantee any token.

1 · Why

Why a token at all?

Live For Ads already pays real USDT from real ad revenue. A token is only worth considering if it adds something to that, rather than replacing it.

Ownership for the community

The people who check in, watch ads and invite friends create the value. A token could give them a real stake in the project they help grow.

Rewards that travel

Today rewards leave the app once every two months as USDT. A token could let rewards be kept, moved or used beyond a single withdrawal window.

Recognise early users

AP, streaks and season history are recorded from day one. A token launch is one way to honour the people who joined while the app was still being tested.

Room for new uses

Possible in-app uses are still being studied, such as boosts, access to special tasks or voting on small product choices. None of these is decided.

The rule we start from

USDT payouts for each season come first and continue. A token must never make existing payouts worse or harder to withdraw.

2 · How

How it would happen, in four stages

Each stage must finish before the next one starts. We publish what we decide at every stage.

NowStage 1

Foundation

Run the app honestly, pay seasons on time, and publish every payment with its transaction link. Keep the leaderboard, season history and AP records clean, because any future token decision would lean on them.

Stage 2

Proof

Pay out several seasons in a row, grow a real and active community, and show that the model works on actual advertising revenue. Start the legal review for the countries we serve.

Stage 3

Design in the open

Publish a draft: what the token is for, total supply, how users could receive it (for example from season AP history), what the team holds and how it is locked, which network it lives on, and how the contract is audited. Collect community feedback before anything is final.

Stage 4

Launch readiness

Final announcement with the date at least 30 days ahead, a security-reviewed contract, a clear claim process inside the app, and a guide on how to stay safe from scams. Only then would a launch happen.

3 · When

When? When these conditions are met, not on a date

We will not name a launch date to create excitement. A date would only be announced after every condition below is checked off.

1

A proven payout record

At least two seasons paid out on time and published in full, with transaction links.

2

A real, active community

Steady daily users and healthy ad revenue, not just a one-time spike in sign-ups.

3

Legal review done

Advice taken for the main countries our users live in, and the plan adjusted to follow it.

4

Security audit passed

The token contract and the claim flow independently reviewed, with the report published.

5

Plan published and discussed

The Stage 3 draft is public for at least 30 days and the community has had time to react.

6

Payouts stay safe

USDT season payouts keep working normally alongside the token plan.

Where we are today: Stage 1. The Testing Season is paying out and Season 0 has begun. The earliest we would even publish a draft is after the first seasons have been paid, and even then it would only be a draft.

5 · Our promises

What we will never do

  • Promise a price, a profit or a guaranteed return.
  • Ask you to send money, a seed phrase or a private key to "claim" or "unlock" anything.
  • Sell tokens privately or run a hidden pre-sale.
  • Launch without a public plan, a published contract and an advance announcement.
  • Make USDT season payouts harder to withdraw because of a token.

Build the record, one check-in at a time

Everything that could matter later starts with being active now.
