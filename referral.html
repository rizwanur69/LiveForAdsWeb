Referral

Invite friends who actually stay

Referrals reward lasting activity, not sign-ups. Your friend must earn their place, and you earn from them for as long as they do.

Eligibility

100 ads + 7 check-in days

Both must be met. The 7 days are total days checked in, not a running streak, so they can be spread out.

One-time bonus

100 AP

Credited once, the moment your referral becomes eligible.

Ongoing

10% commission

Of your referral's AP, for as long as they stay active. Always AP, never UP.

What your Referral page looks like

Each person gets a name, progress lines, a Pending or Eligible badge and a colour bar that moves from deep red through orange to deep green. These are sample users.

The commission figures above are illustrative. In the app, the box in the middle of each entry shows the real total commission from that person, separate from the 100 AP bonus. A summary bar under your link totals Referred, Eligible and AP Earned.
