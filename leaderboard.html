How ranking works in the app

All Time

The default view: lifetime AP, with each person's current streak next to their rank. A missed check-in resets it to 0.

AP and UP views

Two buttons switch to the current season. AP resets with each new season. UP is shown for reference and does not affect AP rank.

Top 100 plus you

Ranks 4 to 100 are listed under the podium. If you fall outside, your own rank still appears in a box beneath the list.
