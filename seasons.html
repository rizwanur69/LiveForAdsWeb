How a payout is built

Season closes

Ad revenue for the whole season is finalised, so the AP allocation shows $0.00 until then.

Window opens

Submit a USDT wallet address on Polygon or opBNB during the two days.

Manual review

Requests are reviewed and approved by the team after the window closes.

USDT is sent

Funds go to your wallet. The season's summary is then published below.
