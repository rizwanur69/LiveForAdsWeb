ads → points → payout
Points build up as you go — but the payout pool only gets calculated once a season ends. There's no instant cash-out per ad. Every point you earn during Testing Season and Season 0 sits in your account until that season closes, at which point it's counted alongside the season's real ad revenue and turned into an actual USDT payout you can withdraw.
The idea

Attention has value. This app pays you your share of it.

Advertisers pay real money to reach Telegram audiences. Live For Ads pools a share of that revenue every season and splits it across the community based on the points each person earned — verified by hand, not just by a script.

How a season works

Four steps, repeated every season

1

Check in & earn

Claim your daily streak, watch ads, and finish tasks — all tied permanently to your Telegram ID.

2

Refer friends

Invite people. Once they hit 25 watched ads, you earn a bonus plus ongoing commission.

3

Season closes

Every two months, the season's ad revenue and community points are calculated together.

4

Withdraw in USDT

The withdrawal window opens for that season, admin-reviewed, then sent via BEP20 or Polygon.
