From first tap to payout in four steps

No download and no new account. The app runs inside Telegram and uses the identity you already have.

Open the Mini App

Launch it from Telegram. Your name and avatar come straight from your account.

Earn every day

Claim your check-in, watch 14-second ads, tick off tasks and try Reward Wall offers.

Climb the leaderboard

AP decides your rank among the top 100. Your streak shows next to your name.

Withdraw your share

When the two-day window opens, submit a wallet and receive USDT after review.

Two currencies, because they are funded differently

Sponsor ads pay out of a shared pool whose size changes every season. Offerwall partners pay a known dollar amount per task. So the app tracks each one on its own.

Your payout is your slice of the season

AP has no fixed price. Once a season closes, its ad revenue is divided by everyone's combined AP. Multiply that rate by your own AP and you have your allocation.

Open payouts and withdrawal window

Built to reward real people

The model is manually verified and designed so that steady, genuine activity beats automated or fraudulent farming.

Tied to your Telegram ID

Progress belongs to your identity, not to a device or a session.

Full-length ad views

An ad only counts if you watch all 14 seconds. Close it early and nothing is paid.

Streak-break reset

Missing a day resets the streak, which favours steady habits over bursts of farming.

Higher referral bar

A referral counts after 100 ads and 7 check-in days, not on sign-up alone.

Manual withdrawal approval

Every payout is reviewed by the team before any funds are sent.

Public payout records

Each season's totals and paid list are published, with wallets partly masked.
