Future outlook

The LFA token

Live For Ads is designed to grow beyond a points-and-payout system. As the community and ad revenue scale, the project intends to explore launching its own on-chain token: LFA.

The same engagement, extended

Watching ads, completing tasks, and referring friends today becomes the foundation for the token economy tomorrow.

Early activity is being tracked

Points and season activity are recorded from day one, positioning early, active members to benefit most from the transition.

This is a forward-looking direction, not a current commitment. No token exists yet, no launch date is set, and nothing here should be treated as a promise of value. If a token launch becomes viable, full details on supply, distribution, utility, and timeline will be published in a dedicated whitepaper update.
