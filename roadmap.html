The LFA token

As the community and ad revenue scale, the project intends to explore launching its own on-chain token. The idea is that the same engagement that earns AP today could become the foundation of a token economy tomorrow. AP, UP and season activity are being tracked from day one.

Not a current commitment

No token exists yet and no launch date is set. Nothing here is a promise of value. If a launch becomes viable, supply, distribution, utility and timeline will be published in a dedicated whitepaper update.
