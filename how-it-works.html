Two kinds of points: AP & UP

Live For Ads tracks two separate balances, because they're funded two different ways.

 AP — Ad PointsUP — USD Points
Earned fromCheck-ins, sponsor ads, tasks, referral commissionOfferwall tasks only
ValueVariable — depends on that season's ad revenueFixed — 100 UP = 1 USDT
Counts towardLeaderboard rank, season payout poolIts own USDT balance only

Why split them? Offerwall partners pay a known, exact dollar amount per completed task — so UP can convert 1:1 at a fixed rate. Ad revenue fluctuates, so AP is pooled and divided by share instead of a fixed rate.

Points system

Where AP comes from

Daily check-in

10–40+ AP, streak-based — resets to 10 if you miss a day.

Sponsor ads

50 AP per ad, across 4–8 slots on the Earn tab.

Tasks

50 AP per task — admin-managed, resets daily.

Referrals

A fixed AP bonus plus ongoing AP commission — referral rewards are AP only, never UP.

Offerwall UP

The only source of UP — a separate, cash-accurate balance at a fixed 100:1 rate.

All points are tied permanently to your Telegram ID — not a device or session — so your progress survives even if you leave and come back later.

Season system

Withdrawal & payout

Getting paid

Withdrawal window

Switched on per season by the admin — typically the 1st–2nd of the month after a season ends.

Review period

Requests are manually reviewed and approved, typically the 3rd–5th.

Payout method

USDT, sent manually via BEP20 or Polygon to your submitted wallet address.

The withdraw form is always visible, but stays off until a season closes. You'll see the withdrawal box on your Profile at all times, but it won't accept a wallet address until the admin switches it on for that season. Once it's live, the in-app message updates to show exactly which season's payout window is open and its dates — set per season from the admin panel, not a fixed monthly date.
No fixed conversion rate. Payout amount comes from that season's allocation calculation — not a pre-set point-to-dollar ratio.
