Anti-fraud and integrity

Seven measures keep the system fair for genuine users.

Persistent identity

Progress is tied permanently to a Telegram ID.

Referral threshold

100 watched ads and 7 check-in days before a referral counts.

Streak-break penalty

A missed day resets the streak, discouraging bursty, farmed activity.

Full-length ad views

Only complete 14-second views count and pay AP.

Time-boxed withdrawals

Wallets are accepted only in the two-day window each season.

Manual approval

Every payout is reviewed before funds are sent.

Public transparency

Season totals and paid lists are published, wallets partly masked.
