Mechanics

Ad-to-earn, in plain terms

Every point traces back to something real: an ad watched, a task done, or a friend who stuck around. Here's exactly how it's calculated.

Watching ads

Short rewarded video ads are served through in-app networks — including Adsgram and Monetag — built to run natively inside Telegram's WebView, so you never get bounced to an outside browser.

Points system

Three ways to earn

Ad views

10 points per ad, up to the daily cap of 10 — reset every day.

Tasks

Fixed one-time rewards, like subscribing to the official channel for 5 points.

Referrals

A fixed bonus plus ongoing commission for as long as your referral stays active.

All points are tied permanently to your Telegram ID — not a device or session — so your progress survives even if you leave and come back later.

Season system

Payouts run on a two-month cycle

Pool calculation happens once every two months, covering that season's full ad revenue and community points together.

Withdrawal & payout

Getting paid

Withdrawal window

Opens on the 1st of each month, for 24 hours only.

Review period

Requests are manually reviewed and approved between the 3rd and 5th.

Payout method

USDT, sent manually via BEP20 or Polygon to your submitted wallet address.

No fixed conversion rate. Payout amount comes from that season's allocation calculation — not a pre-set point-to-dollar ratio.
Integrity

Built to reward real activity

The whole point system only works if it's resistant to bots and farming — here's what keeps it honest.

Persistent identity

Progress is tied permanently to your Telegram ID.

Referral eligibility threshold

A referred account must reach 10 watched ads before it counts.

Daily ad caps

A hard limit of 10 ads per day per user prevents unnatural point accumulation.

Manual approval

Every payout is reviewed by the admin before funds are sent.
