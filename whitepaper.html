01

Abstract

Live For Ads (LFA) is a Telegram Mini App built around a simple idea: users check in daily, watch ads, complete light tasks and refer friends to earn points. At the end of every two-month season, a share of real advertising revenue is pooled and distributed to the community based on each user's point allocation.

The model is transparent, manually verified and designed to reward genuine, active participation rather than automated or fraudulent activity. This version uses a dual-currency system, AP and UP, a renamed Earn page with two sections, and a settings-based Profile with a live AP/UP balance breakdown.

02

The app runs entirely inside Telegram, with no separate download or account beyond your Telegram identity. Revenue comes from partnered rewarded-ad networks and offerwall partners, and a share is paid back to the community that generates it.

03

Daily check-in and streak

The Home page has a check-in box on a weekly cycle, Monday to Sunday. One tap claims the day's AP instantly. Completed days show a checkmark, today's box glows, and upcoming days stay blank.

  • First check-in pays 10 AP, and each following day adds 5 AP (10, 15, 20, 25…).
  • Missing a day breaks the streak, which restarts at 10 AP on the next check-in.
  • The day resets at 00:00 UTC (6:00 AM Bangladesh time).
Why it matters

The streak rewards consistency rather than one-off activity. Resetting to 10 AP keeps the incentive to check in every single day.

04

AP and UP, two point systems

Live For Ads tracks two balances because they are funded two different ways.

AP · Ad PointsUP · USD Points
Earned fromCheck-ins, sponsor ads, tasks, referral commissionOfferwall tasks only
ValueVariable, depends on that season's ad revenueFixed, 100 UP = 1 USDT
Counts towardLeaderboard rank, season payout poolIts own USDT balance only

Offerwall partners pay a known dollar amount per completed task, so UP converts at a fixed rate. Ad revenue fluctuates, so AP is pooled and divided by each user's share.

05

The Earn page

The tab formerly called Ads is now Earn. Two buttons at the top, Reward Wall and Ads, switch between sections that each pay a different currency.

Reward Wall (pays UP). Cash-accurate offerwall tasks such as installing an app, completing a survey or signing up for an offer. Each pays an exact USD amount, credited as UP at 100 UP = 1 USDT. A $0.20 offer credits 20 UP. Gmail or Twitter verification may be required once this section goes live.

Ads (pays AP). Five rewarded-video slots, played through partner networks such as Adsgram or Monetag.

  • 50 AP per ad, with 5 slots per batch (reduced from 10).
  • Each ad must be watched for the full 14 seconds. Closing early is not counted.
  • A 3-hour cooldown starts after the 5th ad and shows as a live countdown on that slot's button.
  • A progress bar below the slots shows how many times each company's ad has been watched.
06

Task system

Under the check-in box on Home sits the task list. Tasks are simple visit-style actions that can be added or removed at any time, and always pay AP.

  • 50 AP per task.
  • Currently live: subscribe to the Telegram channel, visit the website, view the whitepaper.
  • Daily progress is a running counter (for example 0/20) and resets at 00:00 UTC.
07

Where AP comes from

AP is earned through four channels, all tied permanently to a Telegram ID rather than a device or session: daily check-in (10–40+ AP, streak based), sponsor ads (50 AP each), tasks (50 AP each) and referrals (fixed bonus plus 10% ongoing commission).

UP has exactly one source: the Offerwall section of the Earn page. It is never earned from check-ins, sponsor ads, tasks or referrals.

08

Referral program

Referrals reward sustained engagement, not just sign-ups.

  • Eligibility: the referred user watches 100 ads and checks in on 7 separate days (raised from 25 ads).
  • Fixed bonus: 100 AP, credited once the referral becomes eligible (raised from 25).
  • Ongoing commission: 10% of the referred user's AP while they stay active.

The 7-day requirement counts total days checked in, not a running streak. Referral bonuses and commission always pay in AP, never UP.

The Referral page shows each referred user's name with ad and check-in progress, a Pending or Eligible badge, a red-to-green progress bar, a commission box per user, and a summary bar totalling Referred, Eligible and AP Earned.

09

Leaderboard

The Leaderboard opens on All Time, the lifetime AP ranking with each user's current check-in streak beside their rank. Two buttons switch to season views: AP (resets each season) and UP (cash-accurate, shown for reference, does not affect AP rank).

  • The top 3 appear on a podium with gold, silver and bronze crowns and rank-movement indicators.
  • Ranks 4 to 100 are listed below in order.
  • If you are outside the top 100, your own rank, name and value appear in a box beneath the list.
10

Profile and settings

Avatar and display name come from Telegram, with no separate editor. A settings icon holds the Theme switch and Connect Gmail/Twitter (disabled until the Reward Wall requires verification).

Two balance cards show Total AP (check-in, ads, tasks, referral commission) and Total UP (Reward Wall earnings with live USDT value at 100:1), followed by a 7-day chart. Stats offer 7-day, 30-day and all-time views.

A Withdraw button opens the withdrawal page. Below it, each past season has a public payout summary with participant count, total AP, AP allocation in USDT, total UP, UP converted and the combined total. Expanding a season shows the paid list with usernames, partly masked wallets (first and last 5 characters) and USDT withdrawn.

11

The LFA page

A tab labelled LFA, placed just before Profile, opens the public website inside the Mini App so users can read the whitepaper, Terms and Privacy Policy, mechanics and roadmap without leaving Telegram.

12

Live data sync

All tabs stay in sync as you move between them. Watching an ad on Earn, for example, shows up on the Profile AP balance immediately, with no manual refresh or restart.

14

Withdrawal and payout

The wallet-address box is visible year-round but accepts a submission only during a two-day window every two months: 1st at 6:00 AM UTC to the 2nd at 11:59 PM UTC (Season 0 example: November 1–2; following window: January 1–2).

AP allocation. Season ad revenue divided by combined community AP gives a USDT-per-AP rate. Multiply by your AP for your allocation, shown as $0.00 until revenue is finalised.

UP conversion. A swap converts UP to USDT at 100:1, with a Max button. The result adds to the AP allocation so Withdraw shows one combined total with the AP and UP breakdown.

  • Submitted requests are manually reviewed and approved after the window closes.
  • Payout is in USDT to the submitted wallet, on Polygon or opBNB.
AP has no fixed conversion rate

AP payout is set by that season's allocation calculation, not a pre-set point-to-dollar ratio. UP converts at a fixed 100:1 independent of season timing.

15

Anti-fraud and integrity

  • Persistent identity tracking tied to the Telegram ID.
  • Referral eligibility threshold of 100 ads and 7 check-in days.
  • Streak-break penalty that resets to the base value.
  • Full-length ad views: only 14 complete seconds count.
  • Time-boxed withdrawals in a two-day window each season.
  • Manual withdrawal approval before funds are sent.
  • Public payout transparency with partly masked wallets.

The LFA token

The project intends to explore launching its own on-chain token, LFA, as community and ad revenue scale. The same engagement that earns AP today could become the foundation of a token economy tomorrow.

Not a current commitment

No token exists yet, no launch date is set, and nothing here is a promise of value. Full details would be published in a dedicated whitepaper update.
