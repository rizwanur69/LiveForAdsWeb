Abstract

Live For Ads (LFA) is a Telegram Mini App where people earn points by checking in daily, watching ads, finishing partner tasks and inviting friends. Every two months a season ends. A share of the real advertising revenue is pooled and paid to the community in USDT, in proportion to each person's points.

The model is meant to be transparent and fair: payouts are reviewed by the team, every payment is published with its transaction link, and the numbers on this website are read live from the same system that runs the app. Genuine, active participation is rewarded; automated or fraudulent activity is not.

Version 5.0 brings the document in line with the live product: three kinds of earning (Reward Walls, Watch Ads, Shortlinks), the Lucky Spin and Weekly Mystery Chest, one-time tasks, web sign-in with Google or Telegram, a Top 20 leaderboard with season views, support and notices, automatic season rollover, and BEP20 or Polygon payouts. It also adds a plain-language plan for the LFA token: how, when and why.

Accounts and identity

Inside Telegram, your Telegram identity is your account: there is nothing to download and no password. Every balance, referral and withdrawal is tied permanently to that ID, not to a device or session.

  • Web sign-in. Outside Telegram you can sign in with Google, or with Telegram through the bot. A web-only account receives its own permanent numeric ID, and works exactly like a Telegram account in the leaderboard, referrals and withdrawals.
  • Connect Google. A Telegram account can connect to one Google account. A Google account that already has a web-only account cannot be connected to Telegram afterwards.
  • Privacy. Your e-mail is stored separately from your public profile and is never shown to other users.

05

Daily check-in and streak

The Home page has a check-in box on a Monday to Sunday cycle. One tap claims the day's AP instantly. Completed days show a checkmark, today glows, and upcoming days stay blank.

  • Default: the first day pays 10 AP and each following streak day adds 5 AP (10, 15, 20, 25…). The team can change the base, the step and an optional daily cap.
  • Missing a day breaks the streak. It restarts at the base amount on the next check-in.
  • The day resets at 00:00 UTC (6:00 AM Bangladesh time).
Why it matters

The streak rewards consistency over one-off bursts, and it also powers the Weekly Mystery Chest below.

06

Lucky Spin

Every user gets one free spin per UTC day on Home. A spin pays AP straight away, between 5 AP and 250 AP. Small prizes are far more common than large ones, and the team can adjust the prize list. Spins do not carry over: an unused spin is gone at 00:00 UTC.

AP and UP, two point systems

Live For Ads keeps two balances because they are funded in two different ways.

AP · Ad PointsUP · USD Points
Earned fromCheck-ins, spin, chest, ads, shortlinks, tasks, referralsReward Walls only
ValueVariable: depends on the season's ad revenueFixed: 100 UP = 1 USDT
Counts towardLeaderboard rank, season payout poolIts own USDT balance
At season endSaved to your season history, then reset to 0Unwithdrawn UP stays in your balance

Reward Wall partners pay a known dollar amount for each completed offer, so UP converts at a fixed rate. Ad revenue changes from season to season, so AP is pooled and divided by each person's share instead.

09

The Earn hub

The Earn tab is a hub with a sub-page for each source. Every limit, reward and cooldown below is a default that the team can change per network from the admin panel.

Reward Walls 1 to 4 (pay UP). Partner offerwalls with tasks such as installing an app, completing a survey or signing up for an offer. Each offer pays an exact USD amount credited as UP at 100 UP = 1 USDT; a $0.20 offer credits 20 UP. Each wall keeps its own UP total so you can see where your balance came from. The team can switch an individual wall off at any time.

Watch Ads (pay AP). Rewarded videos from partner networks (Adsgram, Monetag, RichAds).

  • Default 50 AP per ad, 5 ads per batch, then a 3-hour cooldown with a live countdown on that network.
  • Partial counts clear at the daily reset (00:00 UTC). A running cooldown always finishes first.
  • Adsterra Smartlinks: these have no "ad finished" signal, so they are paid as a server-checked visit. You open the link, wait at least the minimum time, then press Claim. Each visit works once and expires after 10 minutes. Default 20 AP.

Shortlinks (pay AP). Complete a short link from exe.io or ShrinkMe. Default 100 AP each, 5 per day per provider, with a minimum time check. An unfinished link expires after 30 minutes, and each one can be paid only once.

10

Task system

Tasks sit under the check-in box on Home. They are simple visit-style actions that the team can add or remove at any time. Each task can be completed once per user and pays AP (default 50 AP). Current examples: subscribe to the Telegram channel, visit the website, read the whitepaper.

11

Where AP and UP come from

AP comes from check-ins, the Lucky Spin, the Weekly Mystery Chest, Watch Ads, Shortlinks, tasks and referrals. UP has exactly one source: the Reward Walls. UP is never earned from check-ins, ads, tasks or referrals.

12

Referral program

Referrals reward sustained engagement, not just sign-ups.

  • Eligibility. The referred user watches 100 ads and checks in on 7 separate days (total days, not a running streak).
  • Fixed bonus. 100 AP for the referrer once the friend becomes eligible.
  • Ongoing commission. 10% of the friend's ad AP while they stay active.
  • Adsterra Smartlink visits are weakly verified, so they never count toward "ads watched".
  • Referral rewards always pay in AP, never UP. The team can tune these numbers.

The Referral page shows each referred user's progress, a Pending or Eligible badge, commission earned and a summary of Referred, Eligible and AP earned.

13

Leaderboard

The leaderboard shows the Top 20 earners, with a podium for the top three and each person's current streak. Three views are available: All seasons (lifetime), This season, and any past season. Rank is decided by AP, then UP. If you are outside the Top 20, your own rank is shown beneath the list. Tapping a person opens their public profile (name, photo, cover, AP and UP). Some official profiles can be set to show only name, photo, AP and UP.

Season system

Every season lasts two months and is calculated once, over the full season's ad revenue and community AP. Seasons roll over automatically at 00:00 UTC on the 1st.

At rollover, each person's AP for the finished season is saved to their season history, AP goes back to 0 for the new season, and the season UP counter on the leaderboard restarts. UP you have not withdrawn stays in your balance. The finished season becomes the payout season.

16

Withdrawal and payout

The wallet box is visible all year but accepts a request only inside a two-day window every two months: from 00:00 UTC on the 1st (6:00 AM Bangladesh time) to the end of the 2nd, in odd months (Jan, Mar, May, Jul, Sep, Nov). The team can also open or close the window manually.

AP allocation. The season's ad-revenue pool divided by the season's total community AP gives a USDT-per-AP rate. Multiply by your season AP for your allocation. The AP of a season can be withdrawn only once.

UP conversion. UP converts to USDT at 100:1 with a Max button, and is added to your AP allocation so the Withdraw page shows one total with both breakdowns. A minimum withdrawal can be set by the team.

  • Pay-out is in USDT on BEP20 (BNB Chain) or Polygon, to the wallet you submit. Check the address and network carefully: a wrong network cannot be recovered.
  • Every request is reviewed by the team. BEP20 payments can be sent automatically from the project wallet, within per-payment and daily limits.
  • If a request is rejected, the UP deducted at submission is returned.
AP has no fixed conversion rate

AP is paid according to that season's allocation calculation, not a preset point-to-dollar ratio. UP converts at a fixed 100:1 independently of the season.

17

Transparency

  • Live payments. Every paid withdrawal appears in the Home feed with its transaction link on BscScan or PolygonScan.
  • Season payout boxes. Each season publishes participants, total AP, AP allocation, total UP, UP converted and total paid. The numbers follow real paid withdrawals and are locked a few days after the season closes.
  • Paid lists. Usernames with masked wallets (first and last 5 characters) and the USDT sent.
  • This website. The leaderboard, seasons, withdrawal window and notices here are read directly from the app's data, so they stay current without manual updates.
  • Live data sync. Balances stay in sync between tabs without a refresh.
18

Anti-fraud and integrity

  • Identity is tied to the Telegram ID (or one permanent web ID), not to a device.
  • Referral eligibility needs both 100 ads and 7 check-in days.
  • Visit-style rewards (Smartlinks, Shortlinks) use one-time server sessions, minimum waiting times and daily limits, and a visit can never be paid twice.
  • Daily check-in, spin and chest are limited per UTC day or week and enforced on the server.
  • Streak breaks reset the check-in reward.
  • One AP payout per season per person; UP is deducted at submission so it cannot be spent twice.
  • Time-boxed withdrawal windows, manual review, and a locked payment flow so a request is never paid twice.
  • Banned accounts cannot earn or withdraw, and the team keeps an activity timeline for every user.
  • Public payout transparency with masked wallets.

The LFA token: how, when, why

The project intends to explore launching its own on-chain token, LFA, once the community and the payout record are strong enough. It is a plan, not a promise. The full explanation lives on a dedicated page.

In short
WhyTo give the community real ownership in what it builds, make rewards portable beyond USDT, and reward early, honest users.
